# Retrieval ablation

Generated by `python -m tests.eval.run_ablation` over 123 answerable labelled questions at k=5.

No LLM is involved. An anchor is either present in the retrieved text or it is
not, so these numbers are deterministic and reproduce exactly on any machine
with the same corpus and labels.

| configuration | n | context recall | context precision | hit rate | MRR |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| naive | 123 | 0.935 | 0.236 | 0.967 | 0.833 |
| semantic | 123 | 0.943 | 0.236 | 0.976 | 0.890 |
| hybrid | 123 | 0.927 | 0.229 | 0.967 | 0.849 |
| reranked | 123 | 0.809 | 0.200 | 0.878 | 0.730 |

**context recall** — fraction of a label's anchors appearing anywhere in the top k.

**context precision** — fraction of retrieved chunks that carry an anchor. Falls as k
grows past what a question needs, which is why it is read alongside recall.

**hit rate** — questions where at least one anchor was retrieved.

**MRR** — mean reciprocal rank of the first supporting chunk, counting a miss as 0.
