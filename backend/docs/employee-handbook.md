# Meridian Outfitters Employee Handbook

Welcome to Meridian Outfitters. This handbook covers the policies that apply to all full-time and part-time employees across our Denver headquarters, our Portland distribution center, and our 12 retail locations.

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued at 2 days per month. Part-time employees accrue leave on a prorated basis relative to their scheduled hours.

Leave requests must be submitted through Workday at least 10 business days in advance for absences of 3 or more consecutive days. Shorter absences require 3 business days notice. Your manager must approve all leave before it is considered confirmed.

Up to 5 unused leave days may be carried over into the following year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31 or they are forfeited. Meridian does not pay out unused leave except where required by state law upon termination.

New hires begin accruing leave from their first day but may not take paid leave during their first 60 days of employment except in emergencies approved by HR.

## Sick Leave

All employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, available in full on January 1 (or on the hire date for new employees, prorated). Sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out.

Absences of 3 or more consecutive sick days require a doctor's note submitted to HR within 5 business days of returning to work.

Sick leave may also be used to care for an immediate family member, up to 4 of your 10 annual sick days.

## Parental Leave

Meridian provides 12 weeks of fully paid parental leave for primary caregivers and 4 weeks for secondary caregivers, available after 12 months of continuous employment. Leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption placement.

Employees returning from parental leave are guaranteed their same role or an equivalent role at the same pay grade.

## Remote Work

Corporate employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Tuesdays are designated in-office collaboration days for all corporate teams and cannot be taken as remote days.

Fully remote arrangements are limited to roles explicitly designated as remote in the job description and require VP-level approval. Retail and distribution center roles are on-site only.

Remote employees must be reachable on Slack during core hours of 10:00 to 16:00 Mountain Time and must work from within the United States. Working from outside the country for more than 10 business days per year requires written approval from both HR and Legal.

## Expense Policy

Business expenses must be submitted through Expensify within 30 days of being incurred. Expenses submitted after 30 days may be denied. Itemized receipts are required for any expense over $25.

Meal expenses while traveling are capped at $75 per day ($15 breakfast, $20 lunch, $40 dinner). Alcohol is not reimbursable.

Flights must be booked in economy class through the corporate travel portal at least 14 days before departure when the trip is known that far in advance. Hotel stays are capped at $220 per night in standard markets and $320 per night in high-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, Seattle, Boston, Washington DC).

Mileage for personal vehicle use is reimbursed at the current IRS standard rate. Rideshare is preferred over rental cars for trips under 3 days.

Any single expense over $500 requires written pre-approval from your department head.

## Employee Discount

Employees receive a 40% discount on all Meridian-branded products and 20% on third-party brands sold in our stores, up to $2,500 in total discounted purchases per calendar year. The discount applies in-store and online with your employee account.

The discount is for personal and immediate household use only. Reselling discounted merchandise is grounds for termination.

## Code of Conduct

Meridian expects all employees to treat customers, vendors, and each other with respect. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are prohibited and should be reported to HR or through the anonymous ethics line at ethics.meridianoutfitters.example.

Employees may not accept gifts from vendors valued above $100 per year. Conflicts of interest, including outside employment with competitors, must be disclosed to HR.

## Performance Reviews

Formal performance reviews happen twice a year, in June and December. Merit increases are decided in the December cycle and take effect January 1. Promotion decisions can happen in either cycle.
