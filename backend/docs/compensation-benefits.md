# Meridian Outfitters Compensation and Benefits

This document covers pay, bonus, health coverage, retirement, and the employee discount. The Employee Handbook covers leave, remote work, and expenses, and does not repeat what is here.

## Pay Cycle

Salaried employees are paid semi-monthly on the 15th and the last business day of the month. Hourly employees are paid biweekly on Fridays, covering the two weeks ending the previous Sunday.

Where a payday falls on a weekend or bank holiday, pay is released the preceding business day. Meridian does not delay pay to the following business day, on the reasoning that early is harmless and late is not.

Pay statements are available in Workday within 24 hours of release and are retained for 7 years, matching the retention schedule in the Data Retention and Privacy document.

## Pay Bands and Review

Every role sits in a pay band with a published minimum, midpoint, and maximum. Bands are reviewed annually against market survey data in September, and any band change takes effect January 1.

Individual pay is reviewed once a year in the January cycle. Out-of-cycle increases are permitted for promotion, for a material change in scope, or to correct a pay equity finding, and require director approval.

Meridian runs a pay equity analysis annually before the January cycle. Where the analysis identifies an unexplained gap, the correction is applied in that cycle and is not offset against the employee's merit increase.

## Bonus

Corporate employees at manager level and above participate in an annual bonus, targeted between 8% and 20% of base salary depending on band. Bonus is paid in March for the prior calendar year.

Bonus is calculated on company performance against plan and on individual performance, weighted 60% company and 40% individual. An employee who leaves before the payment date is not eligible, except in the case of retirement, reduction in force, or death, where the bonus is prorated and paid.

Retail store managers participate in a separate quarterly bonus based on store performance, paid within 45 days of quarter close. Distribution center supervisors participate in a safety and throughput bonus paid semi-annually.

## Health Coverage

Coverage begins on the first of the month following the hire date. Employees hired on the first of a month begin coverage that same day.

Meridian pays 85% of the employee premium and 70% of the premium for dependents across all three medical plan tiers. Dental and vision are paid at 100% for the employee and 50% for dependents.

Open enrollment runs the first two full weeks of November for coverage beginning January 1. Changes outside open enrollment require a qualifying life event, reported to HR within 30 days of the event.

Part-time employees scheduled for 24 or more hours per week are eligible for medical coverage at the same contribution rates. Below 24 hours, employees are eligible for dental and vision only.

## Retirement

Meridian matches 100% of the first 3% of pay contributed and 50% of the next 2%, for a maximum match of 4% of pay. The match is applied per pay period rather than trued up annually, so front-loading contributions early in the year reduces the total match received.

Employees are automatically enrolled at 3% after 30 days and may opt out at any time. Contributions vest immediately. The employer match vests on a two-year cliff, meaning employees who leave before two years of service forfeit the match but never their own contributions.

## Employee Discount

Employees receive 40% off Meridian-branded merchandise and 25% off carried brands, subject to vendor restrictions. Some vendors prohibit discounting entirely and those items are marked in the point-of-sale system.

The discount does not stack with promotional discounts. Checkout applies whichever is better for the employee automatically, as described in the Price Match and Promotions Policy.

The discount is for the employee and members of their household. It is not for friends, and it is not for resale. Reselling discounted merchandise is theft under the Code of Conduct and has resulted in termination.

Annual discount purchases above $5,000 at the discounted price are reported to payroll, because the discount above a threshold may be taxable income. Employees are notified before any imputed income is applied.

## Separation

Final pay is issued on the next scheduled payday, or sooner where state law requires. Accrued unused annual leave is paid out where required by state law, and is otherwise forfeited, as stated in the Employee Handbook.

Health coverage continues through the last day of the month of separation. Continuation coverage information is mailed within 14 days of the separation date.
