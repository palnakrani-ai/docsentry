# Meridian Outfitters Travel Policy

This policy covers business travel booking, allowable costs, and reimbursement. The Employee Handbook covers the general expense process, including the 30-day submission deadline and the $25 receipt threshold. Those rules apply here too and are not repeated.

## Approval

All travel requires manager approval before booking. Travel above $2,500 in total estimated cost requires director approval. International travel requires VP approval regardless of cost.

Approval is for the trip rather than for each line item. An employee with an approved trip does not need separate approval for the flight, the hotel, and the meals within policy.

Travel is booked through the Meridian travel portal. Booking outside the portal is reimbursed only where the portal was unavailable or where the out-of-portal fare was materially cheaper, and the employee notes which applied.

## Air Travel

Economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is permitted for flights of 6 hours or more. Business class requires VP approval and is normally approved only for flights over 10 hours where the employee works the day of arrival.

Book at least 14 days ahead where the trip is known that far out. Fares booked inside 7 days require a note explaining why, which is for planning analysis rather than for challenging the employee.

Meridian does not require employees to take significantly inconvenient itineraries to save money. A connection that saves $80 but adds 4 hours is not required, and employees should book the sensible option.

Employees keep airline and hotel loyalty points earned on business travel.

Seat selection fees, one checked bag, and in-flight wifi are reimbursable. Upgrades purchased with personal funds are not reimbursed and are not prohibited.

## Lodging

Book within the portal's displayed cap for the city, which reflects local market rates and is updated quarterly. Where nothing is available within the cap, book the nearest reasonable option and note it.

Meridian does not reimburse short-term rental accommodation booked outside the portal, because of insurance and duty-of-care obligations that a hotel booking satisfies and a private rental does not.

Room service and hotel meals count against the daily meal allowance rather than being separately reimbursable. Hotel laundry is reimbursable on trips of 5 nights or longer.

## Meals

The daily meal allowance is $75 domestic and $95 international, covering all meals and non-alcoholic drinks for the day. The allowance is a cap rather than a per diem, so employees are reimbursed for what they actually spend up to that amount.

Alcohol is reimbursable only at a meal with customers, vendors, or candidates, and is capped at two drinks per person. Alcohol on personal time during a business trip is not reimbursable.

Where a meal is provided by the event or the host, the allowance reduces proportionally. Employees do not claim for a meal they did not buy.

Team meals are claimed by the most senior person present rather than split, which keeps the approval trail clean.

## Ground Transport

Rideshare, taxi, public transit, and airport parking are reimbursable. Rental cars require manager approval and are reimbursed at the mid-size class or below unless the trip requires otherwise, such as a site visit needing cargo space.

Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at the current IRS standard rate, calculated from the workplace rather than from home for trips beginning on a workday.

Traffic and parking fines are never reimbursable, including where incurred in a rental car on company business.

## Duty of Care

Employees traveling internationally register the trip in the travel portal, which is what allows Meridian to locate and assist people if something happens. This is not optional and applies even to a trip booked outside the portal.

Meridian provides travel medical and evacuation coverage for international business travel. The policy number and the 24-hour assistance line are in the portal and should be saved offline before departure.

Employees are not required to travel to a location under a government advisory against travel. Declining such travel carries no career consequence, and a manager who suggests otherwise is subject to the Code of Conduct.

## Personal Travel Attached to Business Trips

Employees may extend a business trip for personal time. Meridian pays the airfare it would have paid for the business-only itinerary, and the employee pays any difference.

Accommodation and meals during the personal portion are not reimbursed. Travel insurance provided by Meridian does not extend to the personal portion, and employees should arrange their own.
