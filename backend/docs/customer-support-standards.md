# Meridian Outfitters Customer Support Standards

This document sets the service standards support agents work to, and the authority each level holds. It does not restate the Returns and Refunds Policy or the Warranty Policy, which govern the decisions themselves.

## Channels and Hours

Email support at support@meridianoutfitters.example operates every day from 06:00 to 20:00 Mountain Time. Live chat operates 08:00 to 18:00 Mountain Time on weekdays. Phone support operates 08:00 to 17:00 Mountain Time on weekdays.

Privacy requests go to privacy@meridianoutfitters.example and are handled by the data team rather than by support, under the Data Retention and Privacy document. Security reports go to security@meridianoutfitters.example under the Incident Response Plan. Agents forward these rather than attempting to resolve them.

## Response Targets

First response within 4 business hours for email, 2 minutes for chat, and 3 minutes hold time for phone.

Resolution within 2 business days for standard enquiries, 5 business days where a warranty assessment or a price match verification is required, and 1 business day for anything involving an order that has not yet shipped, since the window to intervene closes.

These are targets rather than guarantees, and agents are measured on them at team level rather than individually. Individual measurement on response time produces agents who close tickets fast rather than well.

## Agent Authority

Tier 1 agents may, without approval:

- Issue refunds within the Returns and Refunds Policy
- Reship a lost or damaged parcel under the Shipping and Logistics Policy
- Issue store credit up to $50 as a service recovery gesture
- Waive a single restocking fee
- Approve a price match up to $75 of difference
- Extend a return window by up to 14 days

Tier 2 agents may additionally issue store credit up to $200, approve returns up to 30 days outside the window, approve price matches of any amount within the eligibility rules, and authorize a replacement before the original item is returned.

Support managers hold authority above those limits and are the only ones who may approve a refund to a payment method other than the original.

Agents should use their authority rather than escalating within it. An escalation of something the agent could have resolved costs the customer a day and teaches them that the first contact is not useful.

## Tone

Write the way you would speak to someone standing in front of you. Contractions are fine. Apologies are appropriate when Meridian got it wrong and hollow when it did not.

Do not use the passive voice to obscure who did something. "Your order was delayed" hides that Meridian delayed it. Say what happened.

State the decision before the reasoning. Customers reading a refusal want to know the answer first, not to work through a paragraph of context to find it.

Never tell a customer that a policy prevents something when the real reason is that the agent lacks authority. Say that it needs a manager and get one.

## Difficult Interactions

Agents may end an interaction that becomes abusive. The standard is one clear warning, then disconnection, then a note on the account. Agents do not need a manager's permission and are not questioned about the decision afterward.

Abuse means threats, slurs, or sustained personal hostility. Frustration, raised voice, and forceful disagreement are not abuse and are not grounds for disconnection.

Where a customer threatens legal action, agents stop discussing the substance, take the details, and route to the support manager, who involves the general counsel. Continuing to argue the merits after a legal threat helps nobody.

## Service Recovery

Where Meridian caused the problem, fix it and add something. The scale should match the cost to the customer, not the cost of the item.

A delayed order for an ordinary purchase warrants an apology and expedited reshipment. A delayed order that missed a trip warrants more, because the customer lost the use of the item entirely.

Do not offer service recovery for a correctly applied policy the customer dislikes. Doing so teaches customers that persistence changes outcomes, which is unfair to everyone who accepted the first answer.

## Documentation

Every interaction is logged against the account with what the customer asked, what was decided, and why. Support conversations are retained 3 years under the Data Retention and Privacy document.

Agents access only the accounts they are actively working. Looking up any other account, including one's own, is a terminable offence under the Code of Conduct, and access logs are reviewed monthly.
