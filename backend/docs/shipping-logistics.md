# Meridian Outfitters Shipping and Logistics Policy

This document covers how Meridian Outfitters moves orders from the Portland distribution center to the customer. It is the operational companion to the shipping section of the Product FAQ, which publishes rates and delivery windows for customers.

## Carrier Assignments

Standard ground shipments are tendered to Bellweather Freight for destinations west of the Mississippi and to Coastline Parcel for destinations east of it. Expedited and overnight shipments go to Coastline Parcel nationwide, because Bellweather does not guarantee next-day service outside of nine western states.

Oversized items carrying the $49 freight surcharge move on Ridgeline LTL. Ridgeline delivers curbside only and will not carry an item into a building, up stairs, or onto a balcony. Customers who need inside delivery may arrange it directly with Ridgeline at their own cost, and Meridian does not reimburse this.

Alaska, Hawaii, and US territory shipments are tendered to Coastline Parcel for the mainland leg and handed to a regional partner for final delivery. That handoff is why those destinations quote 7 to 10 business days rather than the standard 3 to 5.

## Cutoff Times and Peak Season

The Portland distribution center tenders its last outbound trailer at 15:30 Mountain Time. The customer-facing order cutoff is 13:00 Mountain Time, which leaves two and a half hours of pick, pack, and manifest time.

Peak season runs from November 15 through December 24. During peak, the same-day cutoff moves to 11:00 Mountain Time and standard shipping quotes 5 to 8 business days rather than 3 to 5. The website banner is updated on November 10 each year, five days before the change takes effect.

A second, shorter peak runs from the Friday before Memorial Day through the following Tuesday. The cutoff does not change during this window, but standard delivery quotes extend by one business day.

## Split Shipments and Backorders

An order containing both in-stock and backordered items ships in two parcels at no extra charge. The in-stock portion ships immediately and the backordered portion ships when inventory arrives. Customers are not charged a second shipping fee for the split.

Backordered items are held for 45 days. If inventory has not arrived within 45 days, the backordered line is cancelled automatically and refunded to the original payment method. Customers receive an email at day 30 warning that the cancellation is approaching.

An order that is entirely backordered does not ship and does not charge the customer's card until the first parcel is tendered. Authorization holds placed at checkout expire after 7 days and are reauthorized when stock arrives.

## Address Problems

Addresses that fail carrier validation are held for 3 business days while support attempts to reach the customer by email and phone. If the address is not corrected within 3 business days, the order is cancelled and refunded in full including shipping.

Carriers charge Meridian an address correction fee of $18.50 per parcel when a shipment is rerouted in transit. Meridian absorbs this fee on the first occurrence for any customer. On subsequent occurrences within a rolling 12 months, the fee is passed through and deducted from any refund issued for that order.

We do not ship to PO boxes for expedited or overnight service, because neither carrier guarantees timed delivery to a post office box. Standard shipping to PO boxes is accepted. We do ship to APO and FPO addresses via standard shipping only, with delivery in 10 to 21 business days, and those shipments are not eligible for the lost package reship policy.

## Damaged and Lost Shipments

A parcel is considered lost when the carrier's tracking has not updated for 7 consecutive business days and the carrier's own trace has failed. Meridian files the claim, not the customer. The customer is reshipped or refunded within 2 business days of the trace failing, and Meridian pursues the carrier claim separately.

Visible damage on delivery should be photographed before the packaging is discarded. Damage claims are accepted within 14 days of the delivery scan and require at least two photographs, one of the outer packaging and one of the item. Freight items delivered by Ridgeline must be inspected at the curb and any damage noted on the driver's paperwork, because Ridgeline denies claims that were not noted at delivery.

Reshipments for lost or damaged parcels are sent by expedited service at no charge, regardless of the shipping method originally purchased.

## Returns in Transit

A parcel refused at the door or returned to sender by the carrier arrives back at the Portland distribution center in 7 to 14 business days. Refunds for refused parcels are processed once the parcel is received and scanned, not when the refusal is recorded, and the original outbound shipping charge is not refunded on a refusal.

That differs from a standard return initiated through the returns portal, where outbound shipping is refunded if the return is due to a Meridian error. See the Returns and Refunds Policy for that distinction.
