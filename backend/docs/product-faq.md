# Meridian Outfitters Product FAQ

Answers to the most common questions our customers ask about products, sizing, shipping, and orders.

## Shipping

Standard shipping within the continental United States takes 3 to 5 business days and costs $6.95. Orders over $75 ship free with standard shipping.

Expedited shipping (2 business days) costs $14.95 and overnight shipping costs $29.95. Orders placed before 13:00 Mountain Time ship the same business day.

We ship to Alaska, Hawaii, and US territories via standard shipping only, with delivery in 7 to 10 business days. We do not currently ship internationally.

Tents, kayaks, and other oversized items carry a flat $49 freight surcharge and are delivered curbside in 5 to 8 business days.

## Order Tracking and Changes

You will receive a tracking number by email within 24 hours of your order shipping. Tracking is also available under Order History in your account.

Orders can be modified or cancelled within 1 hour of being placed. After that, the order enters our fulfillment pipeline and cannot be changed; you can return it after delivery under our standard return policy instead.

If a package shows as delivered but you cannot find it, wait 24 hours (carriers sometimes scan early), check with neighbors, then contact support at support@meridianoutfitters.example. We will file a carrier claim and reship or refund lost packages reported within 14 days of the delivery scan.

## Sizing

Meridian apparel runs true to size. If you are between sizes, we recommend sizing up for insulated layers and sizing down for base layers.

Our footwear uses US sizing. Hiking boots in the Ridgeline series run a half size small; we recommend ordering a half size up from your everyday shoe size.

Every product page includes a size chart with garment measurements. Our fit specialists can help by chat 7 days a week from 07:00 to 19:00 Mountain Time.

## Backpack Fitting

Pack sizes are based on torso length, not height. Measure from your C7 vertebra to the top of your iliac crest. Small fits 15 to 17 inches, Medium fits 17 to 19 inches, and Large fits 19 to 21 inches.

All Meridian packs of 40 liters and larger have adjustable torso systems with a 2-inch adjustment range.

## Product Care

Down products (jackets and sleeping bags) should be washed with a down-specific cleaner on a gentle cycle and dried on low heat with dryer balls. Never dry clean down.

Waterproof shells should be washed with technical fabric cleaner and have their DWR coating refreshed every 10 to 12 washes. Tumble drying on low for 20 minutes after washing helps reactivate DWR.

Tents should be stored loosely in the included mesh bag, never compressed long-term, and always dried fully before storage to prevent mildew.

## Gift Cards

Gift cards are available in amounts from $25 to $500, delivered by email or as a physical card. They never expire and carry no fees. Gift cards cannot be redeemed for cash except where required by law, and cannot be used to buy other gift cards.

## Price Adjustments

If an item you purchased at full price is marked down within 14 days of your purchase date, we will refund the difference to your original payment method. Contact support with your order number. Price adjustments do not apply to flash sales, clearance items, or third-party marketplace listings.

## Stock and Backorders

If an item is out of stock, the product page shows an expected restock date when we have one. You can sign up for a back-in-stock email alert on the product page.

Backorders are accepted only for tents and sleeping bags; your card is charged when the item ships, and estimated ship dates appear at checkout. You can cancel a backorder any time before it ships for no charge.

## Loyalty Program

Summit Rewards members earn 1 point per dollar spent. Every 200 points converts to a $10 reward certificate. Members also get free expedited shipping on orders over $150 and early access to seasonal sales. Membership is free; join in-store or at checkout online.
