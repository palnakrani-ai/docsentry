# Meridian Outfitters Product Care and Repair

Meridian repairs what it sells. This document covers the repair service, what it costs, and how repair relates to the warranty. Repair and warranty are different things and are assessed separately.

## Repair Versus Warranty

A warranty claim covers a defect in materials or workmanship and is free to the customer when approved. See the Warranty Policy for coverage and exclusions.

A repair covers damage from use, accident, or wear, which the warranty does not cover. Repairs are a paid service, with the exception of the one free repair per year available to Alpine tier members under the Summit Rewards program.

If a customer submits a repair and the assessment finds a manufacturing defect, the item is converted to a warranty claim and no repair charge applies. The reverse also happens: warranty claims found to be use damage are quoted as repairs, and the customer decides whether to proceed.

## What We Repair

The Portland repair shop handles:

- Zipper replacement on jackets, packs, and tents
- Seam and fabric tears up to 6 inches
- Waterproof membrane delamination
- Buckle, strap, and hardware replacement
- Pack frame and suspension repair
- Tent pole splicing and shock cord replacement
- Down redistribution and baffle repair

We do not repair footwear, technical climbing hardware, avalanche safety equipment, or any item where the repair would affect a load-bearing or life-safety function. Those items are inspected and either replaced under warranty or declined, because a repaired harness or rope is not a repaired item, it is a liability.

We do not repair items from other brands, with one exception: packs and jackets bought from Meridian retail locations before 2019 under brands Meridian no longer carries are still serviced, because the customer bought them from us.

## Cost and Turnaround

Assessment is free. The customer ships the item at their own cost, or drops it at any of the 12 retail locations, and the shop quotes within 5 business days of receipt.

Starting prices are published in the Warranty Policy: zipper replacement from $25, seam and fabric repair from $20, and tent pole section replacement from $15 plus parts. Those are floors rather than typical costs, and the quote reflects the actual work.

Two repair types not listed there:

- Membrane re-lamination: from $70
- Down redistribution and baffle work: from $50

Turnaround is typically 10 business days from receipt at the service center, as stated in the Warranty Policy, and extends to 20 to 30 business days between May 1 and August 31, which is the shop's busy season. Return shipping is free on all completed repairs.

Quotes are held for 30 days. An item whose quote is declined, or not approved within 30 days, is shipped back for the flat $9.95 fee stated in the Warranty Policy.

## Abandoned Items

Items left after a declined quote are held 90 days from the date the quote was declined. Support attempts contact by email at 30 and 60 days.

After 90 days the item is treated as abandoned. Abandoned items in serviceable condition are repaired and donated to the Front Range Gear Library. Items beyond repair are recycled through the materials program described in the Sustainability and Materials document. Meridian does not resell abandoned items, because selling a customer's property is not something a hold period makes acceptable.

## Care Guidance

Waterproof breathable shells should be washed with a technical cleaner rather than detergent, and tumble dried on low to reactivate the durable water repellent finish. Detergent residue is the single most common cause of a shell that appears to have failed but has not.

Down should be washed with down-specific cleaner and dried with clean tennis balls to break up clumps, on low heat, which typically takes 2 to 3 hours. Rushing this with high heat melts the shell fabric.

Tents should be dried completely before storage and stored loosely rather than in the stuff sack. Storing a tent damp is the leading cause of the membrane delamination the shop repairs.

Products returned as defective that show detergent damage, high-heat damage, or storage mildew are assessed as use damage rather than defect. Support explains the finding with photographs from the assessment, because customers reasonably want to see the basis for a declined warranty claim.
