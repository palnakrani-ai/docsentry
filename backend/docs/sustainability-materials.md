# Meridian Outfitters Sustainability and Materials

This document states Meridian's material standards, recycling programs, and published environmental commitments. Claims here are the ones Meridian is willing to be held to.

## Material Standards

All Meridian-branded down is certified to the Responsible Down Standard, covering no live plucking and no force feeding, with traceability to the farm. Certification is verified at each production run rather than once per supplier.

Merino wool is sourced from operations certified non-mulesed. Meridian has not carried mulesed wool since 2021.

Recycled polyester makes up 78% of Meridian-branded synthetic fabric by weight as of the most recent reporting year. The commitment is 100% by 2028. Meridian reports this figure by weight rather than by product count, because reporting by product count would let a few small items misrepresent the picture.

Organic cotton makes up 61% of cotton used. The remainder is conventional cotton sourced through a traceability program. Meridian does not describe conventional cotton as sustainable.

## PFAS and Water Repellency

Meridian eliminated intentionally added PFAS from all production by the end of 2024, as required in the Supplier Code of Conduct.

Current durable water repellent finishes are PFAS-free and perform less well over time than the finishes they replaced. Meridian states this plainly rather than claiming equivalent performance. Reproofing is more frequently needed, which is why the Product Care and Repair document covers reproofing in detail and why the retail locations sell reproofing treatment at cost.

Products manufactured before 2025 may contain legacy PFAS-based finishes. Meridian does not claim otherwise and does not relabel existing stock.

## Packaging

All shipping cartons are minimum 90% post-consumer recycled content and are curbside recyclable. Polybags were eliminated from Meridian-branded apparel in 2023 and replaced with recycled paper wrap.

Carried brands may still arrive in polybags, which Meridian removes at the distribution center and recycles through a film recycling stream. This adds handling cost and is done because the alternative is passing the film to the customer's waste stream.

Void fill is paper. Meridian does not use expanded polystyrene or air pillows.

## Repair and Take-Back

The repair program described in the Product Care and Repair document is the primary sustainability program, on the reasoning that the lowest impact product is the one already made.

Meridian accepts any Meridian-branded item back at end of life at any of the 12 retail locations, regardless of age or condition, at no charge. Items in serviceable condition are repaired and donated to the Front Range Gear Library. Items beyond repair are separated by material and routed to fiber recycling.

Take-back does not earn Summit Rewards points and is not a trade-in. Meridian does not offer credit for returned end-of-life goods, because a credit program would incentivize replacement over repair.

Retail locations recycle their own damaged goods through the same stream, as stated in the Store Operations Manual.

## Carbon

Meridian reports scope 1 and scope 2 emissions annually and has reported scope 3 since 2023. Scope 3 is roughly 94% of the total, which is typical for an apparel business and is the reason Meridian regards scope 1 and 2 reductions alone as insufficient to describe as progress.

The Denver headquarters and the Portland distribution center run on contracted renewable electricity. Retail locations are on renewable supply where the local market allows, currently 7 of 12.

Meridian does not purchase offsets to make a carbon neutral claim and does not describe any product as carbon neutral. The reasoning is that offsets have not proven reliable enough to support a claim customers would read as meaning no emissions occurred.

## Claims Discipline

Marketing claims about materials or environmental performance must be substantiated by documentation on file before publication. The sustainability lead approves every such claim.

Words Meridian does not use in marketing: eco-friendly, green, carbon neutral, plastic free, and sustainable as a standalone adjective. Each is either unverifiable or means too many different things to be informative.

Where a figure is published, the reporting boundary and the year are published with it. A percentage without a boundary is not a claim, it is a decoration.
