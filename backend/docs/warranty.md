# Meridian Outfitters Warranty Policy

Meridian stands behind the gear we make. This document explains what our warranties cover, for how long, and how to make a claim.

## Standard Warranty Coverage

All Meridian-branded products carry a 2-year limited warranty against defects in materials and workmanship, measured from the original purchase date. This covers failures such as delaminating seams, broken zippers, faulty buckles, and stitching that comes apart under normal use.

The warranty applies to the original purchaser only and requires proof of purchase (order confirmation, receipt, or a Summit Rewards purchase record).

## Summit Series Extended Warranty

Products in our premium Summit Series line (identifiable by the Summit Series label on the product and product page) carry a 5-year limited warranty under the same terms as the standard warranty.

Summit Series tents additionally include one free pole replacement within the warranty period, regardless of cause.

## What Is Not Covered

The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, UV degradation, punctures, cuts, abrasion, damage from misuse or improper care (including machine-drying items labeled line-dry, storing gear wet, or dry cleaning down), damage from accidents, modifications, or use of the product for purposes it was not designed for.

Zipper damage from forcing a snagged zipper, and waterproofing loss due to worn DWR coating, are considered wear and tear.

Third-party brands sold at Meridian are covered by the manufacturer's own warranty; we will help you contact them but do not process those claims ourselves.

## How to File a Claim

Submit a claim at meridianoutfitters.example/warranty with your proof of purchase, a description of the issue, and at least 2 photos of the defect. Claims are reviewed within 3 business days.

If approved, we email you a prepaid shipping label. Send the cleaned item to our Portland service center. Items that arrive dirty may be returned unrepaired or incur a $20 cleaning fee.

You can also bring the item to any Meridian retail store and staff will file the claim for you.

## Repair, Replace, or Refund

For approved claims, Meridian chooses the remedy in this order:

1. Repair the item, typically within 10 business days of receipt at the service center.
2. Replace the item with the same model, or the closest current equivalent if the model is discontinued.
3. Issue store credit for the original purchase price if neither repair nor replacement is possible.

Return shipping back to you is always free for approved claims.

## Paid Repairs

For damage not covered by warranty, our Portland service center offers paid repairs on Meridian products: zipper replacement from $25, seam and fabric repair from $20, and tent pole section replacement from $15 plus parts. You will receive a quote by email before any paid work begins, and you can decline the quote and have the item shipped back for a flat $9.95.

## Warranty on Repaired and Replaced Items

Repairs are guaranteed for 90 days or the remainder of the original warranty period, whichever is longer. Replacement items carry the remainder of the original product's warranty, and the warranty period does not restart.

## Claim Status and Turnaround

You can check claim status any time at meridianoutfitters.example/warranty/status with your claim number. End to end, a typical approved claim takes 2 to 3 weeks: 3 business days for review, transit time each way, and up to 10 business days for repair work at the service center.

## International Purchases

The Meridian warranty is honored only for products purchased from Meridian stores, meridianoutfitters.example, or authorized US retailers listed on our dealer page. Products bought from unauthorized resellers, auction sites, or outside the United States are not covered.

## Registering Your Product

Registration is optional and does not affect your coverage, but registering at meridianoutfitters.example/register within 60 days of purchase stores your proof of purchase for you and speeds up future claims.
