# Meridian Outfitters Price Match and Promotions Policy

This policy governs price matching, promotional stacking, and post-purchase price adjustments. It applies to meridianoutfitters.example and to all 12 retail locations.

## Price Match Eligibility

Meridian matches the advertised price of an identical in-stock item sold by an authorized retailer. Identical means the same brand, model, colorway, and size. A different colorway is not an identical item, even at the same manufacturer's list price.

The competing retailer must appear on the approved match list maintained by the merchandising team. That list currently includes eight national outdoor retailers and their websites. Marketplace listings, auction sites, third-party sellers on a marketplace, and members-only warehouse clubs are not eligible, because Meridian cannot verify that those sellers are authorized to carry the brand.

The competing price must be publicly visible and available to any customer without a membership, a coupon code, or a loyalty account. A price that requires signing in is not a public price.

## What Is Excluded

Price matching is not available on the following:

- Items sold as open box, refurbished, scratch and dent, or final sale
- Doorbuster and limited quantity promotions where the competitor states a unit cap
- Prices that appear during a competitor site-wide event lasting less than 48 hours
- Clearance items at either Meridian or the competitor
- Bundle pricing, where the competitor price depends on buying more than one item
- Any item where the competitor stated price is below Meridian's own cost, which merchandising verifies before declining

Requests declined on cost grounds are not explained to the customer in cost terms. Support states that the item is not eligible for a match and offers the current Meridian price.

## How to Request a Match

Online, submit the competitor URL through the price match form before completing your purchase, or within 14 days after. In store, show the competitor website or printed advertisement to any associate. Associates can approve matches up to $75 of difference on their own authority. Differences above $75 require a store manager.

Online requests are reviewed within 1 business day. If approved before purchase, support issues a single-use code valid for 72 hours. If approved after purchase, the difference is refunded to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Post-Purchase Price Adjustments

If Meridian's own price drops within 14 days of your purchase, we refund the difference on request. This is separate from a competitor price match, and the two do not stack on the same item.

Price adjustments are not available on items purchased with a promotional discount of 30% or more, and not on items purchased during the November 15 through December 24 peak window. For peak purchases, a single adjustment window opens December 26 and closes January 10 instead.

An item that has been returned and repurchased at a lower price is treated as a new purchase, and the original purchase is not separately adjusted.

## Promotion Stacking Rules

At most one percentage discount and one dollar-value discount may apply to a single order. A 20% seasonal sale and a $25 welcome credit may be combined. Two percentage discounts may not.

Summit Rewards point redemptions are not discounts for the purpose of this rule and may be applied on top of both. Gift cards and store credit are payment methods rather than discounts, and never count against the stacking limit.

The employee discount does not stack with any promotional discount. Employees receive the better of the two, calculated automatically at checkout. See the Compensation and Benefits document for employee discount rates.

## Advertised Price Errors

Where a price is published in error, Meridian reserves the right to cancel affected orders and refund in full. Merchandising must document the correct price and the duration of the error. Orders already delivered are not clawed back, and the customer keeps the item at the erroneous price.

Errors caught within 4 hours of publication are cancelled without honoring. Errors live for longer than 4 hours are honored for orders already placed, on the reasoning that a price visible for half a day is one a customer could reasonably rely on. That threshold is a Meridian policy choice and is not a legal standard.
