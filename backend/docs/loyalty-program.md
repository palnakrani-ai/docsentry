# Summit Rewards Program Terms

Summit Rewards is the Meridian Outfitters loyalty program. Membership is free and available to any customer with a Meridian account and a US shipping address.

## Earning Points

Members earn 10 points per dollar spent on merchandise, calculated on the amount paid after discounts and before tax and shipping. An order of $120 that used a $20 credit earns on $100, which is 1,000 points.

Points post 14 days after delivery rather than at the time of purchase. The delay exists so that returned items do not generate points that have to be reversed. Points on an item returned within that window are never issued.

Bonus earning applies in these cases:

- Double points during the member's birthday month, applied automatically
- Triple points during member-exclusive events, announced by email at least 5 days in advance
- 500 bonus points for the first purchase made within 30 days of enrolling
- 250 bonus points for adding a verified phone number to the account

Points are not earned on gift card purchases, on shipping charges, on the $49 oversized freight surcharge, or on taxes. Redeeming a gift card as payment does earn points on the merchandise, because the purchase itself is still a merchandise purchase.

## Redeeming Points

1,000 points redeem for $10 of store credit. Points redeem in 1,000 point increments only, and partial increments cannot be redeemed. There is no cap on how many increments may be applied to a single order.

Redeemed credit applies to merchandise only and cannot be applied to shipping, taxes, or the freight surcharge. Credit created from points does not expire once issued, which differs from the underlying points.

Points cannot be transferred between accounts, sold, or combined across household members. Accounts found to be pooling points across unrelated customers are suspended pending review.

## Tiers

Tier is calculated on merchandise spend over a rolling 12 months, not on points balance.

- Base: $0 to $499. Standard earning.
- Ridge: $500 to $1,499. 1.25x earning, free standard shipping with no order minimum, and a 30-day price adjustment window instead of 14.
- Summit: $1,500 and above. 1.5x earning, free expedited shipping, a 30-day price adjustment window, early access to member events, and one free repair per year under the Product Care and Repair policy.

Tier is evaluated on the first of each month. A member who qualifies for a higher tier is upgraded immediately. A member who falls below a threshold retains the higher tier until the next annual anniversary of reaching it, so a single slow year does not cost the benefit mid-year.

## Point Expiration

Points expire 18 months after the month in which they were earned, on a rolling basis. Oldest points are consumed first on redemption.

Any qualifying purchase resets the expiration clock on the entire balance to 18 months from that purchase. A member who buys something at least once every 18 months therefore never loses points.

Members receive expiration warnings by email at 60 days and again at 14 days before points lapse. Expired points are not reinstated except where a Meridian error caused the expiration.

## Account Closure

A member may close a Summit Rewards account at any time through account settings. Points are forfeited on closure and are not converted to credit.

Meridian may suspend or close an account for abuse, which includes systematic purchasing and returning to farm points, creating multiple accounts for one household to stack enrollment bonuses, or reselling redeemed credit. Suspended members keep any store credit already issued, since that credit represents value already earned.

Accounts inactive for 36 consecutive months are closed automatically and points are forfeited. Inactive means no purchase, no redemption, and no account login.
