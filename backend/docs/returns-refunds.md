# Meridian Outfitters Returns and Refunds Policy

We want you to be happy with your gear. This policy explains what can be returned, how, and when you get your money back.

## Return Window

Items may be returned within 14 days of the delivery date for online orders, or within 14 days of the purchase date for in-store purchases. The return must be initiated (return label requested or item brought to a store) within that window.

Summit Rewards members get an extended 30-day return window on all purchases.

During the holiday season, purchases made between November 1 and December 24 may be returned until January 15.

## Return Conditions

Items must be unused, unwashed, and in resellable condition with all original tags and packaging. Footwear must be tried on indoors only; boots with outdoor wear on the soles will be rejected.

The following items are final sale and cannot be returned: clearance items marked "Final Sale," underwear and base layer bottoms, opened fuel canisters, bear spray, food items, and gift cards.

Climbing hardware (ropes, harnesses, carabiners, protection) cannot be returned once the packaging seal is broken, for safety reasons. Unopened climbing hardware follows the standard 14-day window.

## How to Return an Online Order

Start your return at meridianoutfitters.example/returns using your order number and email. You will receive a prepaid return label by email within 1 business day.

A flat $7.95 return shipping fee is deducted from your refund unless the return is due to our error (wrong item shipped, defective on arrival), in which case return shipping is free. Summit Rewards members always return for free.

Online orders may also be returned at any Meridian retail store for free. Bring your order confirmation email and the payment card you used.

## How to Return an In-Store Purchase

Bring the item and your receipt to any Meridian store. Without a receipt we can look up purchases made with a payment card or Summit Rewards account within the last 90 days. If we cannot verify the purchase, we offer store credit at the item's lowest selling price in the last 30 days.

## Refund Timing

Once we receive and inspect your return, refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. Your bank may take an additional 3 to 5 business days to post the credit.

In-store returns with a receipt are refunded on the spot. Refunds for purchases made with a gift card are issued as a new gift card.

Original shipping charges are not refunded unless the return is due to our error.

## Exchanges

The fastest way to exchange is to return the original item for a refund and place a new order. For in-store exchanges, if the new item costs more you pay the difference; if it costs less we refund the difference to your original payment method.

If you need a different size in the same product, use the free size exchange option in the returns portal: we ship the new size immediately and you have 14 days from receiving it to send back the original, with no return shipping fee.

## Damaged or Wrong Items

If your order arrives damaged or incorrect, contact support@meridianoutfitters.example within 7 days of delivery with photos of the item and packaging. We will send a replacement with free expedited shipping or issue a full refund including original shipping, your choice. Do not discard the packaging until the claim is resolved.

## Return Abuse

Meridian monitors return activity. Accounts with excessive returns (more than 10 returns or over 50% of orders returned within any 12-month period) may be limited to in-store returns only, or in extreme cases have return privileges suspended. We will always email you before applying any limit.

## Returning Gifts

Gift recipients can return items with the gift receipt for store credit at the price paid. Without any receipt, gift returns follow the no-receipt process above. Gift returns must still be started within the standard 14-day window (or the extended holiday window when it applies).

## Refused and Undeliverable Packages

Packages refused at delivery or returned as undeliverable are refunded minus the original shipping cost and a $7.95 processing fee once they arrive back at our warehouse.
