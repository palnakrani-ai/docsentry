# Meridian Outfitters Gift Cards and Store Credit

This document covers gift cards, store credit, and the difference between them. Customers frequently treat the two as interchangeable and they are not.

## The Difference

A gift card is a prepaid instrument purchased with money. Store credit is value issued by Meridian, usually from a return, a Summit Rewards redemption, or a service recovery gesture. Gift cards are transferable and store credit is not.

Both function as payment methods at checkout and neither counts against the promotion stacking limit described in the Price Match and Promotions Policy.

## Gift Cards

Digital gift cards are delivered by email within 15 minutes of purchase and can be scheduled up to 12 months ahead. Physical gift cards ship by standard mail at no charge and arrive in 5 to 8 business days. Physical cards are not eligible for expedited shipping, because they ship from the Denver office rather than the Portland distribution center.

Gift cards are available in fixed denominations from $25 to $500, and in custom amounts between $10 and $1,000 online only. Retail locations sell fixed denominations only.

Gift cards never expire and carry no inactivity or service fees. This is a Meridian policy commitment and exceeds what most states require.

A gift card balance cannot be redeemed for cash except where state law requires it. Several states require cash redemption of balances below a stated threshold, commonly between $5 and $10. Support checks the customer's state before declining a cash-out request.

Purchases of gift cards do not earn Summit Rewards points. Spending a gift card does earn points on the merchandise purchased.

## Lost and Stolen Cards

A registered gift card can be replaced if lost or stolen, for the remaining balance at the time the loss is reported. Registration is free and available at any time before the loss.

An unregistered physical gift card cannot be replaced, in the same way cash cannot. Support cannot make an exception to this, because Meridian has no way to establish who holds the card.

Digital gift cards are registered automatically to the recipient email address at delivery, so they are always replaceable.

## Store Credit

Store credit is issued to the account it belongs to and cannot be transferred, gifted, or converted to a gift card. It cannot be redeemed for cash under any circumstances, including the state law provisions that apply to gift cards, because store credit was never purchased with money.

Store credit from a return expires 24 months after issuance. Store credit from a Summit Rewards point redemption does not expire. Store credit issued as a service recovery gesture expires 12 months after issuance and support states the expiry at the time it is issued.

Where an account holds multiple credits with different expiry dates, the credit expiring soonest is consumed first.

## Applying Both to One Order

An order may use any number of gift cards and any amount of store credit together. The checkout applies store credit first, then gift cards, then the remaining balance to the payment method on file. Applying credit first protects the customer, because store credit expires and gift cards do not.

If an order paid partly with a gift card is refunded, the gift card portion is refunded to a new gift card and the remainder to the original payment method. If an order paid partly with store credit is refunded, the credit portion returns to store credit and retains its original expiration date rather than resetting.

## Fraud Controls

Meridian limits gift card purchases to $2,000 per account per rolling 7 days. Orders exceeding that are held for manual review, which takes up to 2 business days.

Gift cards purchased with a payment method that is later charged back are voided, including any remaining balance. Where the card has already been spent, the merchandise order is cancelled if it has not shipped and referred to the fraud team if it has.

Customers who report being asked to pay a third party with Meridian gift cards should be told plainly that this is a scam. Meridian never requests payment by gift card, and no legitimate agency does. Support escalates these reports to the fraud team even when no Meridian order is involved.
