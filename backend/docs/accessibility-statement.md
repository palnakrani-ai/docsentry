# Meridian Outfitters Accessibility Statement

This statement covers accessibility of the Meridian website, the retail locations, and Meridian as an employer. It states what is done, what is not yet done, and when.

## Website

The Meridian website targets WCAG 2.2 Level AA. The most recent third-party audit was completed in the current reporting year and covered the home page, category and product pages, cart, checkout, account management, and the returns portal.

Known gaps as of that audit:

- The size chart modal traps keyboard focus on three legacy product templates. Fix scheduled for the next quarter.
- Two promotional banner variants fall below the 4.5:1 contrast ratio. These are being retired rather than corrected.
- The store locator map has no non-map equivalent. A text list of locations with addresses and hours is in development.

Meridian publishes known gaps rather than claiming full conformance. A statement that claims conformance while gaps exist is worth less than one that names them.

Anyone who encounters a barrier can contact accessibility@meridianoutfitters.example. Reports are acknowledged within 2 business days and Meridian states a fix date or explains why one is not yet possible.

Where a barrier prevents completing a purchase, support completes the order on the customer's behalf by phone at the price and promotions available online, including any online-only offer.

## Retail Locations

All 12 retail locations have step-free entry. Nine have accessible fitting rooms meeting current standards, and the remaining three are scheduled for renovation, two in the coming year and one the following year.

Assistance animals are welcome in all locations. Staff are trained not to ask what task the animal performs, and not to ask for documentation.

Portable induction loops are available at the service desk in all locations. Staff are trained to offer them rather than waiting to be asked, since asking places the burden on the customer.

Quiet shopping hours run Sunday from 11:00 to 13:00 at all locations, with reduced music, lower lighting, and no announcements.

## Products

Product pages state closure types, since zip pulls, magnetic closures, and toggles differ substantially in usability. Meridian is adding this to legacy product pages and expects to complete the backfill within the year.

Meridian carries a small adaptive range with magnetic closures and seated-fit outerwear. The range is smaller than Meridian would like and expanding it is a stated priority rather than a completed commitment.

## Employment

Meridian provides reasonable adjustments through the hiring process and in employment. Candidates are told about the adjustment process at the first contact rather than being left to ask.

Adjustment requests go to HR and do not require medical documentation in the first instance. Documentation is requested only where the adjustment is significant and the need is not evident.

Interview formats are adjusted on request, including extended time, written questions in advance, and alternative formats for skills exercises. The Hiring and Onboarding document requires the same core questions for every candidate, and adjusting the format does not conflict with that.

The physical requirements review for distribution center roles is conducted by an occupational health provider rather than by the hiring manager, which keeps the assessment about the actual requirements of the job.

## Feedback and Review

This statement is reviewed twice a year and after any significant site change.

Meridian tests with assistive technology users rather than relying only on automated tools. Automated scans catch a meaningful fraction of issues and miss most of the ones that actually block a task.

Where a fix date is stated here and then missed, the revised date and the reason are published rather than the date being quietly changed.
